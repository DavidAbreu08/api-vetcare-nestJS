# Animal tutors

## Animal tutors

An animal has any number of active tutors (`vc_animal_owners`, migration `1795435200000`) and at most one active primary tutor (filtered unique index); while it has active tutors one of them is primary (application rule, enforced in a transaction holding the animal lock). Rows are immutable periods (`assigned_at`, `ended_at`, `assigned_by`, `ended_by`, `end_reason` TRANSFERRED/REMOVED/ROLE_CHANGED/ACCOUNT_DELETED/MIGRATED): a tutorship is ended, never deleted; promoting/demoting closes the row and opens a new one. `vc_animals.owner_id` is kept as a synchronized copy of the active primary tutor (same transaction); every access decision uses `vc_animal_owners` through `AnimalAccessService` (`isActiveTutor`, `isPrimaryTutor`, `activeTutorIds`, `canReadAnimal`, `canManageAnimal` and the `activeTutorExists(...)` SQL fragment, an `EXISTS` on `uq_vc_animal_owners_active`). A tutor must be an active account holding CLIENT (400 otherwise). The 409 names below (`ALREADY_TUTOR`, `ANIMAL_HAS_NO_PRIMARY`, `PRIMARY_TUTOR_NOT_REMOVABLE`, `ALREADY_PRIMARY`, `TUTOR_HAS_OPEN_RESERVATIONS`, ...) are internal rule names; the HTTP body only carries `errorMetadata` and a readable `friendlyMessage`.

| Route | Access | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GET /api/vetcare/animal/:id/owners | staff, active tutors | active tutors `[{ user, isPrimary, assignedAt }]`, primary first; `?includeHistory=true` adds closed periods (`endedAt`, `endReason`; ADMIN also `assignedBy`/`endedBy`) for staff and the primary tutor only (403 otherwise) |
| POST /api/vetcare/animal/:id/owners `{ userId }` | staff, primary tutor | adds a secondary tutor; 409 `ALREADY_TUTOR` (also when `uq_vc_animal_owners_active` rejects a concurrent insert, 2601), 409 `ANIMAL_HAS_NO_PRIMARY` when the animal has none |
| DELETE /api/vetcare/animal/:id/owners/:userId | staff, primary tutor, the secondary tutor themselves | ends a secondary tutorship (REMOVED); the primary tutor cannot be removed (409 `PRIMARY_TUTOR_NOT_REMOVABLE`: transfer first). `transferFutureReservations` in the body or query string (`true`/`false`) |
| POST /api/vetcare/animal/:id/transfer `{ newPrimaryUserId, keepPreviousAsSecondary?, transferFutureReservations?, reason? }` | ADMIN | closes the current primary period (TRANSFERRED), closes the new tutor's secondary period if any, opens the new primary period, optionally reopens the previous primary as secondary, updates `owner_id`; no consent of the new tutor; 409 `ALREADY_PRIMARY`; with `keepPreviousAsSecondary: true` 409 `PREVIOUS_TUTOR_UNAVAILABLE` when the previous primary's account no longer exists and 409 `PREVIOUS_TUTOR_NOT_CLIENT` when it is inactive or no longer holds CLIENT (nothing is changed; resend without the flag). Also assigns a primary to a legacy animal without one |

- Reads: the animal (`GET /api/vetcare/animal`, `/:id`, `/owner/:ownerId`), its measurements and its clinical visits (without clinical notes) are visible to staff and every active tutor; `ownerId` filters (GET /api/vetcare/animal, /owner/:ownerId) mean "active tutor (primary or secondary)". Former tutors lose access to the animal, measurements, visits and reservations; the clinical record follows the animal (a new tutor sees earlier visits). Animal responses keep `owner` (the primary tutor) and add `tutors`.
- Edition: staff and the primary tutor (secondary tutors 403). Species/breed approval rules are unchanged; staff who are an active tutor of the animal (primary or secondary) follow the client path (no immediate approval). `PATCH /api/vetcare/animal/:id` with `ownerId`: the current primary is a no-op (compatibility); another account is a transfer for ADMIN (same transaction, with history; never moves reservations: 409 `TUTOR_HAS_OPEN_RESERVATIONS`, use POST /transfer with the flag) and 403 for everyone else.
- Reservations: any active tutor may book; the reservation's `client_id` is the responsible tutor, who must be an active tutor of the animal (pre-check 400, re-checked under the animal and client locks: 409 `ANIMAL_OWNER_MISMATCH`). Every active tutor sees the animal's reservations and their history; only the reservation's client or ADMIN cancels. The client of a reservation keeps seeing that reservation after leaving the animal (they stay responsible for it); they no longer see the animal's other reservations.
- Leaving with open reservations (PENDING/CONFIRMED not yet finished, as in "Integrity and lifecycle") of the animal whose client is the leaving tutor: 409 by default (IN_USE; the message states how many open reservations the tutor is the client of; no id list — use GET /api/vetcare/reservations?animalId=...&clientId=...); with `transferFutureReservations: true` their `client_id` moves to the new primary tutor (transfer) or to the current primary tutor (removal of a secondary; 409 `PRIMARY_TUTOR_INACTIVE` when that account is inactive) in the same transaction, each with a `CLIENT_CHANGED` history event (`changedBy` = caller, `oldData { clientId }`, `newData { clientId }`, transfer `reason`). The success `result` of DELETE is `{ movedReservationIds: string[] }`, empty when none was moved (OpenAPI schema `RemoveAnimalOwnerResponseDto`; data, not an error detail).
- Accounts: DELETE /api/vetcare/users/:id is refused while the account is the active primary tutor of non-deleted animals (409 IN_USE; the message states how many animals; find them with GET /api/vetcare/animal?ownerId=...); its active secondary tutorships are closed in the same transaction (ACCOUNT_DELETED). Removing the CLIENT role (PUT /api/vetcare/users/:id/roles) applies the same rule (ROLE_CHANGED) and is also refused (409 IN_USE `TUTOR_HAS_OPEN_RESERVATIONS`, count in the message) while the account is the client of open reservations of animals it is an active secondary tutor of: cancel them or end the tutorship with `transferFutureReservations: true` first (deletion already refuses any open reservation as client). Deactivation (`isActive=false`) does not change tutorships: it is reversible and an inactive account cannot sign in; while inactive it cannot be made tutor, receive moved reservations or have new reservations (existing rule `CLIENT_INACTIVE`). Primary rows of soft-deleted animals are left untouched.
- Note (tutors of soft-deleted animals): deleting an animal does not close its tutorship rows; its active primary row stays open but neither blocks account deletion/CLIENT removal nor grants access (animal reads exclude soft-deleted animals), and its secondary rows are closed with the account like any other. Known behaviour, unchanged for now.
- Concurrency guard: every close of a tutorship row (removal, transfer, account deletion, CLIENT removal) is an `UPDATE ... WHERE ended_at IS NULL`; a period closed by a concurrent flow is never rewritten. In removal/transfer, 0 rows affected returns 409 CONFLICT (`TUTORS_CHANGED`, "Tutors changed; reload and retry") and rolls back the whole transaction.
